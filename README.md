# Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92): one line, at most 280 characters — the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) scores 10 fresh candidate lines against the judge's five criteria (breakup text from a self-aware toaster at breakfast, genuinely funny twist, toaster's own voice, clear specific reason for leaving, one tight line), picks the winner, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates the 1–280 character one-line gate.

**The line (102 chars):**

> The crumb tray holds 47 of your promises to clean me. I toasted your last bagel this morning. Goodbye.

Why this one: the proven winner shape from the vacuum-alibi final — a specific machine object as evidence locker (the crumb tray holding counted, broken promises), a counted detail (47), and a reversal in the last clause (the final bagel is already toasted; the goodbye is a fact, not a threat).
